NORTHSTAR TECHNOLOGIES
WORKPLACE CONDUCT AND COMMUNICATION POLICY
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 1 January 2026

1. PURPOSE
Northstar Technologies expects employees to contribute to a respectful, safe, inclusive, and professional workplace. This policy describes expected conduct and how workplace concerns should be raised.

2. RESPECTFUL BEHAVIOUR
Employees must treat colleagues, customers, suppliers, and visitors with courtesy and respect. Harassment, bullying, intimidation, discrimination, threats, and retaliation are not acceptable.
Disagreements should be handled professionally. Employees should focus on work-related issues and avoid personal attacks.

3. PROFESSIONAL COMMUNICATION
Employees should use clear, respectful language in meetings, email, messaging tools, and work-related online spaces. Confidential business information must be shared only with authorized recipients.
Employees should use company communication channels for business matters and follow applicable information-security requirements.

4. MEETINGS AND COLLABORATION
Meeting organizers should provide an agenda when practical and invite only people whose participation is useful. Participants should arrive on time, contribute constructively, and respect others' opportunity to speak.
Employees who cannot attend an important meeting should notify the organizer when possible and review any relevant notes or assigned actions.

5. CONFLICTS OF INTEREST
Employees must disclose actual or potential conflicts of interest to their manager or HR. Examples include participating in a decision involving a close relative, using company information for personal gain, or having an outside activity that interferes with job responsibilities.
Employees must not use their role to obtain improper personal benefits.

6. USE OF COMPANY RESOURCES
Company equipment, accounts, facilities, and funds must be used responsibly and in accordance with applicable policies. Limited personal use may be permitted when it does not interfere with work, create additional cost, or violate security requirements.
Employees must not use company resources for unlawful, abusive, or unauthorized activities.

7. RAISING A CONCERN
Employees may raise a workplace concern with their manager, HR, or the designated reporting channel. If the concern involves the employee's manager, the employee may contact HR directly.
Reports should include relevant facts, dates, people involved, and available supporting information. Employees should cooperate with a fair review.

8. CONFIDENTIALITY AND NON-RETALIATION
Concerns will be handled as discreetly as practical, subject to the need to review the matter fairly. Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith or participates in an investigation is prohibited.
Knowingly making a malicious or deliberately false report may itself be addressed under company procedures.

9. POLICY VIOLATIONS
Reported violations may be reviewed by HR and the appropriate business or compliance representatives. Any action will follow company procedures and applicable law.

10. QUESTIONS
Employees may contact HR through the employee portal for guidance on workplace conduct, conflicts of interest, or reporting a concern.
