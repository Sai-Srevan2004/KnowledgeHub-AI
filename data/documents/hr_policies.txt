NORTHSTAR TECHNOLOGIES
HUMAN RESOURCES POLICY
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 1 January 2026

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This policy describes key human resources processes at Northstar Technologies. It applies to regular full-time employees unless a section states otherwise. Employment contracts and applicable local law take precedence if they provide different requirements.

2. RECRUITMENT
Hiring decisions are based on role requirements, relevant skills, experience, and structured interview feedback. Interviewers must record job-related feedback in the approved recruitment system.
Candidates must not be asked for information that is unrelated to the role or prohibited by applicable law. Any employee involved in a hiring decision must disclose a personal or financial conflict of interest.

3. JOINING AND ONBOARDING
New employees receive an onboarding plan from their manager and HR. The plan normally includes an introduction to the team, role expectations, required training, company policies, and access to approved work systems.
Employees must complete mandatory security, conduct, and workplace-safety training within the time communicated by HR.

4. PROBATION
New regular full-time employees normally have a probation period of six months, unless their offer letter states a different period.
During probation, the manager should provide feedback on role expectations, work quality, collaboration, and development needs. HR may coordinate a review before the probation period ends.
Successful completion of probation is confirmed in writing by HR. If additional assessment time is required, HR will communicate the next steps and any applicable extension in writing.

5. PERFORMANCE REVIEWS
Managers are expected to discuss goals and performance with employees regularly. A formal performance review is normally conducted once each year.
The review considers agreed objectives, quality of work, collaboration, professional conduct, and development progress. Employees may add comments to their review record.

6. LEARNING AND DEVELOPMENT
Employees may request role-related training through their manager. Approval depends on business need, available budget, and the relevance of the training to the employee's role.
Mandatory compliance and security training must be completed by the assigned deadline.

7. INTERNAL TRANSFERS
Employees interested in another internal role should discuss the opportunity with their manager and apply through the internal careers portal. Selection depends on the role requirements and the internal recruitment process.

8. EMPLOYEE RECORDS
Employees must keep their contact details, emergency contact information, and bank or tax details up to date in the HR system. Access to employee records is restricted to authorized personnel with a legitimate business need.

9. RESIGNATION AND EXIT
Employees who resign must submit written notice through the process described in their employment agreement. HR and the manager coordinate handover, return of company property, and removal of system access.
Employees must return company devices, access cards, documents, and other company property by the date communicated by HR.

10. QUESTIONS
Employees should contact HR through the employee portal for questions about onboarding, probation, performance reviews, employee records, or exit procedures.
