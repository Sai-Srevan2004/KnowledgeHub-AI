NORTHSTAR TECHNOLOGIES
EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 1 January 2026

1. PURPOSE
This policy explains how employees request reimbursement for approved business expenses. It applies to employees who incur expenses on behalf of Northstar Technologies.

2. ELIGIBLE EXPENSES
Reasonable expenses that are necessary for an approved business activity may be reimbursed. Examples include approved business travel, client meeting costs, role-related training approved in advance, and required business supplies.
Personal purchases, traffic fines, personal entertainment, and expenses without a clear business purpose are not reimbursable.

3. PRIOR APPROVAL
Employees must obtain manager approval before making a non-routine purchase or committing to a business expense that exceeds the spending limit assigned to their team.
Training, conference registration, and equipment purchases require written manager approval before booking or purchase.
An approval does not guarantee reimbursement if the expense violates this policy or lacks required documentation.

4. SUBMITTING A CLAIM
Employees must submit an expense claim through the company expense portal. Each claim must include:
- The transaction date.
- The amount and currency.
- The business purpose.
- The relevant cost centre or project, when applicable.
- An itemized receipt or invoice.
- Evidence of prior approval when required.

Claims should normally be submitted within 30 calendar days after the expense is incurred. If an employee cannot meet this deadline, the employee must contact Finance and explain the delay.

5. RECEIPTS AND MISSING DOCUMENTATION
An itemized receipt is required for each expense unless Finance confirms an exception in writing. A bank or card statement alone may not provide enough detail to establish what was purchased.
If a receipt is unavailable, the employee must provide a written explanation and any alternative supporting evidence. Finance decides whether the claim can be processed.

6. REVIEW AND APPROVAL
The employee's manager reviews the business purpose and confirms that the expense was appropriate. Finance checks the claim for policy compliance, required documents, coding, and duplicate submissions.
Employees must not approve their own expense claims. Claims involving a manager's personal expenses must be approved by that manager's designated approver.

7. REIMBURSEMENT
Approved claims are included in the next available reimbursement payment cycle. Processing time may vary depending on the completeness of the claim and the payment schedule.
Finance may return an incomplete claim to the employee for correction.

8. PROHIBITED PRACTICES
Employees must not split a purchase into multiple claims to avoid an approval threshold. Employees must not submit altered receipts, duplicate claims, personal expenses, or expenses already paid by another party.
Suspected fraud must be reported to Finance or the appropriate compliance contact.

9. QUESTIONS
Questions about claim status, receipts, expense categories, or reimbursement processing should be submitted through the Finance support channel in the employee portal.
