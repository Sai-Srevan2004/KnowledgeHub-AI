NORTHSTAR TECHNOLOGIES
BUSINESS TRAVEL POLICY
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 1 January 2026

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This policy sets expectations for employees travelling on approved company business. It covers trip approval, booking, accommodation, local transport, travel safety, and expense claims.

2. TRAVEL APPROVAL
Employees must obtain written manager approval before booking business travel. The request should state the business purpose, destination, proposed dates, estimated cost, and project or cost centre.
International travel may require additional approval from the department head and any review required by the company's security or legal teams.
Employees must not assume that a travel request is approved until the required approver has confirmed it.

3. BOOKING
Employees should use the company-designated travel booking channel where available. Travel should be booked early enough to obtain reasonable fares while meeting business requirements.
The lowest available fare is not automatically the best option if it creates unreasonable travel times, safety concerns, or prevents the employee from meeting the business purpose.
Personal upgrades and optional leisure extensions are the employee's responsibility unless explicitly approved in writing.

4. AIR AND RAIL TRAVEL
Employees should select a practical economy-class option for standard business trips unless an exception is approved in writing by the department head.
Employees should retain booking confirmations and proof of payment for their expense claim.
Changes or cancellations caused by business needs should be discussed with the manager and travel support channel as soon as possible.

5. ACCOMMODATION
Employees should choose safe, reasonably priced accommodation close to the business destination where practical. The booking should comply with any location-specific spending guidance issued by Finance.
Personal services, minibar purchases, entertainment, and unrelated guest costs are not reimbursable.

6. LOCAL TRANSPORT
Employees should use safe and reasonably priced local transport. Public transport, approved ride services, or rental vehicles may be used when appropriate for the destination and business schedule.
Parking, tolls, and local transport costs must be supported by receipts or other documentation required by Finance.

7. TRAVEL SAFETY
Employees must review relevant travel advisories and follow company safety instructions. Employees should keep their manager informed of significant itinerary changes.
An emergency, accident, illness, lost travel document, or safety concern during a business trip must be reported to the manager and the appropriate local emergency service where necessary.

8. COMBINING BUSINESS AND PERSONAL TRAVEL
An employee may request to combine personal travel with a business trip. The manager must approve the arrangement in writing before booking.
The company will reimburse only the costs that would have been incurred for the approved business itinerary. Additional personal travel, accommodation, meals, and insurance are the employee's responsibility.

9. EXPENSE CLAIMS
Travel expenses must be submitted under the Expense Reimbursement Policy. Claims must include receipts, the business purpose, travel dates, and evidence of required approvals.
Employees must not claim personal expenses as business travel costs.

10. QUESTIONS
Employees should contact the travel support channel or Finance through the employee portal for questions about bookings, travel approvals, or travel expense claims.
