KnowledgeHub AI — Additional Policy Documents

This package contains four additional fictional company-policy documents:
- it_policies.txt
- hr_policies.txt
- finance_reimbursement_policy.txt
- travel_policy.txt
- workplace_conduct_policy.txt

Use these alongside your existing data/documents/employee_handbook.txt.
That gives you six documents total if you keep the existing handbook.

All company names, policy details, timelines, and procedures in these sample documents are fictional and intended only for RAG learning and evaluation. They are not legal or HR advice.

Setup:
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Copy the five .txt files into KnowledgeHub-AI/data/documents/.
3. Keep your existing employee_handbook.txt unchanged.
4. Run your existing ingestion pipeline and check the generated chunk count.
5. Do not change chunk_size or chunk_overlap yet; we want to establish an evaluation baseline first.
