# Employee Handbook

## Parental Leave
Eligible employees receive 16 weeks of parental leave. Employees should submit parental leave requests through the HR portal at least 30 days before the expected leave date when possible.

## Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 20 days of annual leave per calendar year. Leave requests must be approved by the employee's manager.

## Remote Work
Employees may request remote work arrangements with manager approval. Some roles may require employees to work from the office.

## IT Support
Employees experiencing account access issues should contact the IT help desk. Employees must never share their passwords with anyone.
